# Privacy Policy for intoDNS Quick Inspector

Last updated: September 2026

intoDNS Quick Inspector ("the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how your information is handled.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
intoDNS Quick Inspector does **not** collect, store, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, user credentials, browsing history, or analytical data.

### 2. Permissions and Data Handling
- **Active Tab Permission (`activeTab`):** When you explicitly click the extension action button, the extension temporarily reads the domain name of your currently active browser tab solely to redirect you to its corresponding intoDNS report (`https://intodns.com/<domain>`). This data is processed locally in memory and is never saved, logged, or transferred to any remote server.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The extension opens public DNS diagnostic reports on intoDNS (intodns.com). Any interaction with intoDNS once redirected is governed by intoDNS's own terms and privacy practices.

### 4. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can reach out via the extension's official GitHub repository issues page.
